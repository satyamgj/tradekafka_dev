Non Flow Trades:

The objective is to make sure that we are the single point of truth for all trades done by a bank/broker. For this we need to make sure that manual data entry of the trades done outside the system by the broker/bank is also fed into our system as a bulk upoad/single entry. This will make our CCIL report more accurate and the chances of them accepting the system is going to be higher.

Step 1: Save the non flow trades:

This will be a screen that looks like deal entry with 2 sections cut horizontally.

Section 1 is the trade parameters (The financial parameters)
Section 2 is the commission parameter. This section should ideally be independent of the upload file, and should be autofilled with the option of editing if the user wants. This will be autofilled from the entity/user level config setting.

	Scenario:
	
	Kanjii gets one buy order from ICICI for 1m USD/INR
	
	Now in user/Entity setting we have the following commission rate information:
	
	Commission Bible ------- Kanji takes 600 INR for every 1 million USD traded from each side.
	Calculated Commission: 10M USD/INR --- 600*10 ICICI + 600*10 HDFC = 12000 INR
	
	Kanji is doing 1.5 billion USD trade --- 1500 Million. 1500*1200 INR = 18,00,000 INR everyday!!!!
	Cash: 60*10*1500 = 1,80,000 Per Day
